Our Story

"Every jar tells a story of tradition, care, and authentic flavors."

At Nakshatra Pickles & Home Foods, we believe that the best flavors come from the heart. Every jar of pickle, every batch of sweets, and every pack of snacks is prepared using time-honored recipes passed down through generations.

We use only the freshest ingredients sourced from local markets, combined with traditional spices ground at home. Our commitment to quality means absolutely no artificial preservatives — just pure, homestyle goodness.

From the fiery Avakaya mango pickle to the delicate sweetness of Ariselu, every product tells a story of tradition, care, and authentic Telugu cuisine.
